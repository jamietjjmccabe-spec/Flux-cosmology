# Legacy and Exploratory Model Status

The repository preserves earlier work for provenance. Presence in the repository does not imply current endorsement as physical evidence.

## Historical phenomenology

The JWST Metric Archive package, galactic Closure D work, registration-depth gravity notes, and metric-memory dark-matter documents are retained as historical phenomenological investigations. They are quarantined from the active quantum-to-classical bridge because no covariant stress tensor has been derived from record formation.

## Cosmology scripts

Files such as `cmbr.py`, `cmbr4.py`, `fit_w_cpl.py`, `flux_class_driver.py`, and related background modules demonstrate scalar-field or effective-fluid compatibility calculations. They do not derive cosmic acceleration from quantum actualisation and must not be cited as evidence for MIP.

Any banner claiming that a toy background is a proven deeper replacement for \(\Lambda\)CDM is superseded by the 23 July 2026 framework statement.

## Variable-constant and local-gravity scripts

Lunar, black-hole, and unified toy models that vary \(G\) or \(c\) are retained as demonstrations. The lunar recession result is driven by calibrated standard tidal torque. The black-hole stacking factor is a reinterpretation of Schwarzschild redshift.

## Astrophysical and visual toy models

Jet, accretion, galaxy, stellar, three-body, and map scripts remain exploratory numerical visualisations unless a separate audit document demonstrates dimensional consistency, convergence, and comparison with a standard null model.

## Cross-domain universal Flux

The psychological/social/attention version of Flux is an analogy and is no longer part of the physics framework.

## ECEM/ECEE planetary battery

The planetary-battery code and outputs remain a separate heuristic exoplanet-ranking project. Its manually weighted scores are not probabilities, measurements, or tests of MIP physics.

## Rule for future use

Legacy files may be reused only when their assumptions are restated and their status is made explicit. New papers and README summaries must point first to `docs/2026-07/CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_2026-07-23.md` and `docs/2026-07/BRANCH_STATUS_LEDGER_2026-07-23.md`.
